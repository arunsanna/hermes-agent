## Verdict

The earlier configuration change enabled Copilot in the Hermes profile, but **did not make Copilot a supported Switchboard Hermes provider**. The earlier task outcome saying Copilot was configured for new Hermes sessions must be read as a Hermes ACP/profile result, not proof of Switchboard creation or iPhone UI support.
